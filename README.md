# SGAN
time-series attack
Due to their inherent vulnerabilities, deep neural networks are susceptible to adversarial sample attacks, posing potential security risks. Currently, adversarial attack algorithms in the field of time series classification are mostly based on white-box attacks. However, the more practically relevant black-box attack algorithms suffer from low efficiency, weak adversarial sample attack capability, and poor concealment of adversarial samples. To address these issues, this paper proposes a local time series black-box attack method based on Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs). Firstly, adversarial perturbations are generated based on GANs, and a substitute classification model is used in training to implement the black-box attack without accessing the internal information of the target model. Secondly, time series shapelets are utilized as local perturbation intervals to enhance the attack capability of adversarial samples while improving their concealment. Finally, on the basis of local perturbations, an adaptive learning method is used to adjust the magnitude and smoothness of data perturbation, further enhancing the attack capability and stealth. Experimental results on the UCR dataset, which has potential security vulnerabilities, show that this method can efficiently generate adversarial samples with a high success rate of attack. Compared to baseline algorithms, the mean square error between adversarial and real samples is lower, significantly improving the imperceptibility of the adversarial samples.
